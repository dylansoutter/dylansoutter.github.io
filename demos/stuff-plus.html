Stuff+ illustrative demo

This project trains an XGBoost classifier on 2020–21 Division I fastball-family pitches to estimate the chance of a called or swinging strike (CSW). It tests that model on 2022 pitches, then converts average predicted CSW into a pitcher-team Stuff+ grade.

  1. 1. Physical inputsRelease, spin, movement, and release-angle measurements from each pitch.
  2. 2. XGBoost predictionThe selected model estimates CSW probability without batter, count, sequence, or plate location.
  3. 3. Stuff+ scalePitch predictions are averaged by pitcher-team and placed on a 100-centered reference scale.

Illustrative demo: Every profile, measurement, and score below is invented. Switching profiles does not run XGBoost or produce a real scouting evaluation.

Spin axis is encoded as sine and cosine; horizontal measurements are mirrored to a common handedness. The selected model excludes adjusted approach angles after a holdout comparison. In the actual project, 100 is based on qualifying 2022 D1 pitcher-team averages, and 10 points represents one standard deviation of those averages. A score does not measure command or guarantee an outcome.
